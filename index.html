Shard Explorer recreates a classic online fantasy world from its original 1998 map data, rendered in modern real-time 3D. Ground, wall and roof materials, trees, plants, rocks and props come from public-domain (CC0) photo scans by Poly Haven and ambientCG; buildings are assembled procedurally, and the layout of land, towns and dungeons comes from the map files.
